# Bar tutorial and service flow

The replayable tutorial creates an isolated practice order for Mia: a Gin & Tonic. It supplies the ingredients, confirms the specific order through English dialogue, opens the preparation counter and follows the recipe quest. The player drags bottles from the shelf, adds mixers and garnish, then serves the order.

Serving does not credit the sale. The player asks about payment in the conversation, receives the payment and a guaranteed practice tip, closes the dialogue and collects the tip jar. Next, Storage → Market teaches adding a Tonic bottle to the cart and placing an order. Practice delivery instantly refills the consumed tonic; real deliveries retain their normal delay. Practice actions never save account rewards or consume account stock. Finishing or skipping restores the original account state.

The practice guest has no visible timer or patience bar, and the practice clock is frozen. Regular customers and empty-seat invitation timers are hidden throughout the entire tutorial, including the steps after the tip. They become visible only after Start playing or Skip tour.

All paid drinks, food and sealed bottles follow dialogue payment. An unpaid guest remains in the bar and cannot be dismissed, replaced with a similar order or sold another pitch. Payment is credited once; tips remain in the jar until collected.

The header uses a centered portrait, bar level and name, compact balances, Events and second-row Bar info and Mail buttons. Phones display two guest positions, with arrows and a red dot for guests outside the visible range. Bartender position can be dragged horizontally or adjusted with arrow keys and is saved for each interior. The tip jar sits near the right edge.

The practice guest is constructed without random traits: Mia always orders Gin & Tonic and pays in cash. The first question is How can I help you?, followed by Would you like a Gin & Tonic?, then Would you like to pay by card or in cash?. Training word banks use sentence order without shuffled distractors. A late account connection updates the saved real state while practice stays offline.
