# Bar tutorial and service flow

The replayable tutorial creates an isolated practice order for Mia: a Gin & Tonic. It supplies the ingredients, confirms the specific order through English dialogue, opens the preparation counter and follows the recipe quest. The player drags bottles from the shelf, adds mixers and garnish, then serves the order.

Serving does not credit the sale. The player asks about payment in the conversation, receives the payment and a guaranteed practice tip, closes the dialogue and collects the tip jar. Practice actions never save account rewards or consume account stock. Finishing or skipping restores the original account state.

All paid drinks, food and sealed bottles follow dialogue payment. An unpaid guest remains in the bar and cannot be dismissed, replaced with a similar order or sold another pitch. Payment is credited once; tips remain in the jar until collected.

The header uses a centered portrait, bar level and name, compact balances, Events and a second-row Bar info button. Phones display two guest positions, with arrows and a red dot for guests outside the visible range. Bartender position can be dragged horizontally or adjusted with arrow keys and is saved for each interior. The tip jar sits near the right edge.
