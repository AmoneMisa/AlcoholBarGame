# Painted keepsake and wheel artwork

Created using the built-in imagegen tool. All assets have transparency and are installed as WebP. Source PNG paths are recorded in `scripts/painted-keepsake-art.json`.

Shared style: polished hand-painted 2.5D mobile game asset for an amber-lit luxury cocktail lounge; centered full object, transparent background, warm gold reflections, dimensional materials, no flat vector art, emoji, outlines, badge or text.

- **book**: An open ivory-page leather-bound book, deep navy cover with gold corners, slightly angled three-quarter view.
- **flowers**: A small lush bouquet of pink roses and ivory blossoms with green leaves, wrapped in cream paper tied with a burgundy silk bow.
- **sweets**: Three exquisite individually wrapped chocolate bonbons in burgundy and gold foil, arranged as a small gift cluster.
- **vinyl**: A glossy black vinyl record half emerging from a midnight blue album sleeve with subtle gold art deco ornament, no text.
- **watch**: A luxurious gold wristwatch with deep blue dial and brown leather strap folded to show a three-quarter view, no readable numbers.
- **wheel**: A perfectly circular front-facing prize wheel with EXACTLY TWELVE equal 30-degree wedge panels. Sector dividing spokes at 12 o'clock then every 30 degrees clockwise. Alternating midnight navy, teal, burgundy and deep amber enamel panels, visibly painted rich enamel lighting and fine textures. Elegant sculpted gold rim with tiny jewel studs, small centered gold hub. Completely empty panels: no words, numbers, pictures, symbols or prizes, no pointer. Perfectly radial symmetric wheel, intended as a rotating game sprite.
