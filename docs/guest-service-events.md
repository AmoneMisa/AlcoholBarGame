# Ordinary guest service events

Smokers can request an ashtray, then ask for it to be emptied and cleaned or removed after use. Cleaning at the seat keeps the tray in front of the guest. Removing transfers it to the dirty stock; washing returns it to the clean stock. Removing it twice is rejected, and leaving after removal does not count the same tray again.

Active requests appear on the guest card and in the conversation, including requests that arise after the conversation has opened. Water, taxi and conversation requests remain available; a helpful conversation fulfills a chat request. Special situations keep their dialogue choices and normal frequency.

Hungry guests may request a named dish, ask for a recommendation or let the bartender choose a suitable pairing. Recommendations use the confirmed cocktail or selected bottled alcohol before serving, and the last drink afterward. Available food is ranked by pairing and excludes known allergies. The dialogue shows suitable dishes and their serving buttons. A requested dish that is unavailable can be replaced through an explicit alternative offer.

Food can be served before the alcohol. Its cost joins the drink bill, the drink remains preparable, and the combined bill is collected once through the payment dialogue. Paying for food separately keeps the guest's drink order. Payment problems continue to occur with combined orders and hold the full amount.

Regression coverage includes ashtray requests and stock conservation, all three food-request variants, food before alcohol, combined dialogue payment, spirit pairing, known allergies, water and chat requests, and a payment problem with a combined bill.
