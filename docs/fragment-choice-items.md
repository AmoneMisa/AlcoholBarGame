# Fragment choice items

Four independent inventory items: Style fragment choice, Background fragment choice, Circle fragment choice and Equipment fragment choice. Each confirmed selection consumes one item and adds exactly one fragment to that target's own pile. No common fragment pool is introduced. Items are only available through drops, gifts or administered rewards; they cannot be bought for zero crystals.

Exact chest chances PER CATEGORY: bronze 0.1%, silver 0.2%, gold 0.4% (combined 0.4%, 0.8%, 1.6%). Existing drops share the remaining probability. Choice chests can offer these through their existing silver/gold pool.

The common eligibility function is used by the selection UI and server. All past and future configured Battle Pass seasons, achievement costumes and their connected backgrounds, and event backgrounds and connected costumes are excluded. Friends who have already joined cannot consume another invitation fragment choice. Server rejects missing items, invalid category/target pairs and bypassing the selection through Use or Buy.

The inventory inspection popup shows a searchable illustrated grid and Confirm fragment stays disabled until a target is selected. The existing Storage items popup shares the same selector. Closing does not consume anything.
