# The Circle character art prompts

Final art was made with the built-in ImageGen tool. Each character was generated separately as a transparent, full-figure seated adult for a painterly semi-realistic 2D cocktail-bar game. Shared instructions: three-quarter pose on an invisible stool, looking toward the viewer; visible hands and legs; warm dramatic bar light; clean silhouette; centered with transparent margin; no background, text, or watermark. Card portraits are crops of those figures.
